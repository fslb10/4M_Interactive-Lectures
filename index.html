Plan your teaching time

Short estimates include the spoken core at 125 words/minute, all seven complete recordings, response pauses and transitions. Context/Meaning discussions and optional extensions add time. Full ranges are classroom planning estimates, not measured delivery times. Full 1.12 uses a 60–70-minute planning allowance for this release; its approved guide leaves discussion flexible.

The 1.10 and 1.11 short lessons need 13:50 and 14:41 because their recordings alone take 6:39 and 7:30. Choose the version for your class; do not infer duration from slide count.

Present and use offline

Download an HTML lesson and open it in a browser. Recordings, captions, passage explanations, illustrations, notes and the unit map are embedded. External source citations require internet. Home returns to the dashboard when the files remain in this folder structure; a separately downloaded lesson remains usable without it.

Use arrows, Space or Page Up/Down to navigate. N opens notes; O opens all slides; U opens the unit map; F toggles fullscreen; Escape closes a panel. P plays/pauses and R replays Scripture; CC toggles captions. Opening Context or Meaning pauses audio and keeps the position. Close the panel, then press Play to resume.
