Using the lessons

Select a gold reference to play or pause; CC toggles subtitles. Context and Meaning open optional passage discussions, pause audio and keep your place. Close, then press Play to resume. N opens notes, O opens all slides, U opens the unit map.

Short estimates include the complete spoken core script at 125 words/minute, full Scripture audio, two 30-second response pauses and transitions. Full plans include discussion and activities. Neither is a measured classroom duration. Extra discussion adds time.
