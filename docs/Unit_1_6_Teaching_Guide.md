# Unit 1.6 One True Baptism

This 20-slide lecture follows the supplied **Unit 1.6.docx**. Its central understanding is that Baptism is Christ’s sacrament of new birth by water and the Spirit: it unites us to his saving death and Resurrection and begins a lifelong Christian life. Extended explanation, listening questions, sources and suggested answers are built into the presenter notes.

| Slides | Purpose | Coverage of the supplied note |

## Catholic teaching clarifications

The lecture preserves the note’s Catholic emphasis and refines several broad statements:

- The necessity of Baptism is taught together with desire, blood and hope in God’s mercy. Neither automatic final salvation nor automatic condemnation of an unbaptized person is taught. See [CCC 1257–1261](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_one/article_1/vi_the_necessity_of_baptism.html).
- Baptism forgives sin and gives a permanent character, while temptation and certain consequences of the Fall remain. Later sin can obstruct the fruits of grace. See [CCC 1263–1274](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_one/article_1/vii_the_grace_of_baptism.html).
- The Latin baptismal formula is identified as Latin; the Church also has an Eastern liturgical form. Immersion and pouring are presented as the ordinary forms of celebration. See [CCC 1239–1243](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_one/article_1/iii_how_is_the_sacrament_of_baptism_celebrated.html).
- Validity in other communities is assessed through the sacramental requirements, not simply a denomination’s name. Conditional Baptism concerns a serious remaining doubt after inquiry. Baptism precedes valid reception of the other sacraments. See [canons 842, 849, 854 and 869](https://press.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann834-878_en.html).
- Parents, godparents and the whole Church nurture growing faith. Sponsor eligibility involves more than being baptized. See [CCC 1246–1255](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_two/section_two/chapter_one/article_1/iv_who_can_receive_baptism.html) and canon 874 in the linked Code.
