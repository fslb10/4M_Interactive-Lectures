# Scripture audio publication record

Reviewed 15 October 2026. The teacher changed the requested delivery from local classroom files to the existing public GitHub Pages site. This release covers only the twelve published 20-slide lessons.

The [publisher's website policy, section II](https://www.friendshippress.org/pages/nrsvue-quick-faq), permits qualifying website quotations under its gratis limits. This release treats the connected educational presentations as a website quotation collection. The same page contains overlapping exclusions for new media/software; this interpretation is not an individualized license or a general authorization to redistribute the Bible text.

The selected audio contains 437 distinct verses, 502 verse occurrences across lessons, and no complete biblical book. Repeated verses are counted once in the distinct website inventory. Selected Scripture represents approximately 18% of substantive lesson-and-reading words across the collection; individual lesson estimates range from 12% to 24.2%. These figures exclude navigation and repeated audio/transcript representations. Existing short quotations also remain in the teaching content. Recheck the inventory and publisher's conditions before expanding the collection or changing its use.

The prescribed NRSV-CE notice appears in each lesson's Notes and Help panels and on the linked credits page. Its stock permission wording is not evidence of a personal permission letter. No individualized grant was requested or obtained, and no fee was accepted.

The recording manifest identifies the exact verses, source URLs, narration segments, voices and MP3 hashes. Scripture wording is preserved. Recordings omit spoken reference/translation introductions; short internal editorial cues identify a jump between selections.

Kokoro v1.0 uses Apache-2.0 model weights. Only generated recordings are embedded; the model and runtime are not redistributed. Voice licensing does not supply rights to the Scripture text.
